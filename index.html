How the odds are worked out

Each friend's chance is their yes rate, blended with the group's average as if they'd already had 5 typical invites. A new friend starts near average and moves toward their real rate as you log more. Recent invites count more: an invite loses half its weight every 90 days. A flake counts as a no, and a bit heavier than one.

Pick a kind of plan and the same blend runs on just those invites, starting from that friend's overall chance.
